## Open-source licences {#credits}

Brouk is **open source** under the Apache License 2.0: anyone may use, change and share it, for personal or work use. Its source code is at [github.com/DreaddCZ/brouk](https://github.com/DreaddCZ/brouk).

Brouk is built on other open-source software: Chromium (through CEF), the libraries it uses for settings, history, encryption and updates, the ad blocking engine with its Rust crates, and the password strength estimate. Their licences ask that their notices go along with Brouk, and here they are.

**Open them:** Help → **Open-Source Licences**, or type `brouk://credits` in the address bar. The page opens in a tab:

- **Brouk's own licence** is at the top.
- **Every component** follows with its version and licence; click one to read its licence texts. **Project page** opens the project's website in a tab.
- **Search** narrows the list by name or licence, for example `MIT` or `regex`.
- **Chromium credits** (on the Chromium Embedded Framework entry) lists the hundreds of projects inside Chromium itself. **Full notices** (on the Rust standard library) shows the notices of the Rust standard library.

Everything on the page is part of the app, so it works offline. About Brouk points here too.
