Find and zoom (spec 012)

Find: ⌘F, type beetle. Expected: "1/5"; Return or ⌘G goes to the next match, Shift+Return or ⇧⌘G to the previous one, Escape closes the bar. Switching tabs and back keeps the bar and its text.

The beetle (Czech: brouk) is an insect. A beetle has hard wings. Some beetle species are tiny; the largest beetle is huge. Every beetle loves bugs.

Zoom: ⌘+ and ⌘- change the zoom in Chrome's steps (110 %, 125 % …); the address bar shows the percentage; clicking it or ⌘0 resets. Reopen this page in a new tab: the zoom is remembered for the site. Trackpad pinch magnifies the page.
