## Everyday browsing {#web-basics}

Brouk follows Chrome for everything web pages do; this lists what is worth knowing.

### Site permissions {#permissions}

When a site asks for the camera, microphone, screen sharing, location, notifications or similar, Brouk asks under the address bar: **Allow** or **Block**, remembered for that site **in that profile**. The site information button (left of the address) shows the site's permissions and connection and lets you change them.

**Screen sharing:** Brouk shows its own picker of screens and windows. The first time, macOS asks to allow screen recording for Brouk (System Settings → Privacy & Security).

### Pages and windows {#pages}

- **Find in page:** <kbd>⌘F</kbd>, <kbd>⌘G</kbd> / <kbd>⇧⌘G</kbd> for next and previous.
- **Zoom:** <kbd>⌘+</kbd>, <kbd>⌘−</kbd>, <kbd>⌘0</kbd>; remembered per site in each profile, shown in the address bar when not 100 %.
- **Print** (<kbd>⌘P</kbd>), including Save as PDF. PDFs open in the tab.
- **Full screen:** pages (videos, slides) can go full screen — <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves; <kbd>⌃⌘F</kbd> makes the window full screen.
- **Popups** a page opens without a click are blocked; an icon in the address bar lets you allow them for the site.
- **Links to apps** (`mailto:`, `msteams:`, `zoommtg:`, `slack:`…) ask before opening the app, with "always allow for this site".
- **Crashes:** a crashed tab shows a page with **Reload**; a page that stops responding offers to wait or close it. Other tabs keep working.
- Signing in to company sites with Windows authentication or a client certificate from your keychain works as in Chrome.
- Spelling is checked in text fields with macOS's languages (Settings → Pages).
